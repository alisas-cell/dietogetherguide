## Can the 560 supplied candidates presently yield +500?

**Not established, and the supplied set as presently qualified cannot yield 500 without replacements beyond that set or a material change in evidence/intent.** Even a deliberately overgenerous upper bound, approving every candidate other than the **115 duplicate-intent routes in this review alone**, is **560 − 115 = 445**. This ignores all other cluster failures, all 61 needs-evidence here, and all replacement-pool duplication, so it is a capacity bound rather than a realistic achievable count. This is not a claim that 500 quality pages are impossible forever. New documented systems, dated releases, original verified observations and substantial independent utilities can create new page intents. A relabeled clone cannot. The previous review's pool answers mostly repeat existing or primary questions; do not treat the extra 60 paths as 60 spare independently qualified pages.

The correct immediate claim is a **bounded qualified expansion**, with page drafts passing gates individually. To establish 500 later requires a materially larger evidence/intent inventory and requalification, not a lower word-count threshold, giant unknown-stat tables, generic troubleshooting variants or additional route shells.
